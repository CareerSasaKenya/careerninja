## Rules applied

- Shifts are **12-hour** Day (D) and Night (N).
- Minimum **12-hour gap** between a person’s shifts (no Night → next-morning Day; no double-shift on the same calendar day).
- Each member has **6 Rest (OFF) days**, except **Emmanuel** who has **7 OFF** days covering approved leave.
- **Rachel** and **Winniestella**: Day shifts only.
- **Emmanuel leave**: 3–5 and 18–21 August.
- **Moses leave**: 2 August (counts as one of his 6 OFF days).
- Every shift has **4 or 5** people (almost all shifts have 4; 31 Aug Day has 5).
- Night/Day mix is balanced as evenly as possible for flexible staff. Because Rachel and Winniestella take only Days, the other eight cover all Nights, so they naturally work somewhat more Nights than Days (~9–11 D / ~14–16 N).
